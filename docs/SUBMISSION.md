## Title

Walk With Me

## Short description

Record a little of yourself. Let your echo light the way. A small four-room puzzle about keeping yourself company.

## Full description

You thought you were walking alone, until your own past footsteps lit a bridge ahead.

In **Walk With Me**, record up to twelve seconds of movement, then cooperate with your echo. Leave it on a pressure plate, meet it halfway, or record a quiet moment of waiting. Your echo follows the footsteps you kept, then stays where you left it.

Four small rooms, one gentle idea, and a trail of little lights at the end. Designed for a first-time play of about 5–8 minutes; that estimate is pending independent human timing. No ads, accounts or online features. Sound is optional.

**Languages:** English (first-launch default) and Simplified Chinese. Use the top-right language button to switch at any time; your preference is saved locally.

**Controls:** WASD / arrows to move. R to start or finish recording. T to reset. Esc to pause. Bridges linger for 2.4 seconds after their plates release. Watch their flashing stripes.

**Windows download:** extract the whole ZIP, then open `Walk With Me.exe`. Requires Windows 10/11 x64, .NET Framework 4.8 and the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Evergreen Runtime (normally already installed). No Node, local server or internet is needed to play. The executable is unsigned. A supplementary browser version is included separately.
