Walk With Me / 回声同行

A four-room puzzle about keeping yourself company. Record your footsteps, then cooperate with an echo of your past self.

Human entrant: 何添雨 · TapTap studio: RainStar工作室 · Prepared for thatgamejam #02, November 4, 2026. This document describes the build; it does not assert successful event submission.

Windows build and startup

Extract the entire Windows x64 ZIP and run Walk With Me.exe inside its folder. Keep the accompanying game directory and DLLs together. Windows 10/11 x64, .NET Framework 4.8 and Microsoft Edge WebView2 Evergreen Runtime are required. The app is unsigned. Once prerequisites are installed, no Node.js, server or network connection is needed to play.

The optional TapTap H5 edition supports landscape touch controls. It supplements the Windows build. First launch defaults to English; use the language button for full Simplified Chinese.

Controls and rules

Move
WASD or arrow keys. On phones, use the direction buttons.
Record
R starts or finishes a recording; maximum 12 seconds. Starting clears the old echo and restores the initial room. Finishing restores all room state and begins playback.
Echo
One echo follows your timed path without collision solving, then holds its final position indefinitely. It does not loop. Both actors activate pressure plates and pass through one another; only the player can finish a room.
Bridges
They remain for 2.4 seconds after plates release. Flashing stripes and a countdown show this grace period. If a bridge vanishes beneath the player, the attempt resets.
Other controls
T resets the room; Esc pauses. Losing focus also pauses replay time and clears input. Resume explicitly. Sound can be muted.

Click Begin, solve four rooms and reach each exit. Room 1 teaches leaving an echo on a plate. Room 2 adds two plates together. Room 3 adds a recorded wait between two plates. Room 4 combines waiting with meeting your echo. The ending lights the footsteps you left behind. Use How to play for progressive hints and full routes.

Complete gameplay video: a continuous, unaccelerated, silent browser capture using actual keyboard input, from startup through all four rooms to the ending (about 60 seconds with a known solution). It is not a first-time play-duration measurement or a physical-phone recording. Target first-time play is 5–8 minutes; independent timing remains pending.
